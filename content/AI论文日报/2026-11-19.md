### [Neural Memory Orchestration](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23857v1)

- **原标题**：Neural Memory Orchestration
- **来源与时间**：arXiv · 2026-11-18T05:02:56+00:00
- **作者/分类**：Michael Zizov、Boris Morose · cs.DC、cs.LG、cs.OS
- **来源摘要**：Modern HPC workloads increasingly stall on the memory hierarchy rather than on compute, and page faults from mmap'd files, swap, and tiered memory are a growing fraction of wall time. Heuristic prefetchers in the kernel handle stride-like and temporally clustered patterns well but degrade sharply on the irregular, data-dependent access streams typical of these workloads. Learned prefetchers have been shown to capture such patterns in simulation but have not been taken to a deployed pipeline running against live workloads on real hardware. We present Overseer, an end-to-end learned page-prefetching system. An eBPF collector observes page faults on the target host, a transformer predictor \(OverNet\) runs on network-attached inference hardware and emits future page addresses, and a kernel-resident enforcer materializes those pages into the target's address space before they are demanded. OverNet encodes fault sequences through per-instance ordinal ranks over deltas, which lets a single trained model serve any target process without retraining. On two page-fault-heavy workloads, Overseer reduces runtime by roughly 35% on Hilbert-curve traversal and by roughly an order of magnitude on dense matrix multiplication, with corresponding reductions in major-fault counts.

### [Beyond Cross-Modal Reconstruction: Participant-Level Evaluation of sEMG-Kinematic Coupling in Post-Stroke Gait](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23856v1)

- **原标题**：Beyond Cross-Modal Reconstruction: Participant-Level Evaluation of sEMG-Kinematic Coupling in Post-Stroke Gait
- **来源与时间**：arXiv · 2026-11-18T05:02:17+00:00
- **作者/分类**：Rafsan Jany、Md Rafid Islam、Mohammad Fahim Islam、Hafiz Abdur Rahman · cs.LG
- **来源摘要**：Accurate cross-modal reconstruction is often treated as evidence of physiological coupling, although it may simply reflect temporal patterns shared across participants. We tested whether frozen bidirectional models captured subject-specific neuromuscular coupling between electromyographic activity and gait kinematics in 82 held-out able-bodied and 43 post-stroke participants. Complete source-modality removal and cross-participant source substitution, along with a cycle-position-age-sex template baseline, were applied. Learned bilateral asymmetry was also compared with transparent waveform and derivative asymmetry for predicting Performance-Oriented Mobility Assessment \(POMA\) scores in the case of repeated nested cross-validation. Complete source removal increased reconstruction RMSE by 6.90% for electromyography-to-kinematics and 11.38% in the reverse direction. In contrast, cross-participant substitution increased RMSE by only 1.68% and 1.67%, respectively, while the template reduced RMSE by 2.89% and 0.55%. For POMA prediction, walking speed, age, and sex achieved an MAE of 3.693, compared with 4.229 and 4.427 after adding absolute and signed learned asymmetry, respectively. In all of the 20 repeated evaluations, all asymmetry representations performed worse than the covariate baseline. No localized learned feature remained significant after covariate adjustment and false-discovery-rate correction. The model mainly relied on shared gait-phase patterns, with no clear subject-specific coupling or added clinical value. Participant-level controls are therefore needed before reconstruction accuracy is treated as evidence of individualized physiology.

- **来源摘要**：Can AI agents design robots that work in the real world? We introduce RoboBuilder, a benchmark in which coding agents co-design a robot's hardware and program from standard parts, motors and a custom 3D-printed part, and every design that passes in simulation is built and tested on a physical course. Simulation alone is not enough: under public part data, none of 15 initial passing designs crossed the real course, so an agent learns the simulator from measurements it requests and videos of real runs. Across four frontier coding agents, ten obstacles at three difficulty levels, 16,427 simulations of 12,774 distinct robots and 135 physical robots built in an estimated 240 hours, agents solve nearly all easy obstacles and invent mechanisms our search baselines do not reach, but falter at harder levels, when one robot must cross every obstacle, and in the real world, where 66% of simulated passes hold across all levels. Closing the loop helps: shown a video of its real failure, an agent redesigns the robot, sometimes correcting its simulator, and removes 72% of level-1 and level-2 failures. RoboBuilder measures progress toward agents that engineer real machines. Project Page: https://ghadinehme.com/robobuilder.github.io/

- **原标题**：A Fast, Programmable GPU-to-CPU Invocation Layer for GPU-Initiated Communication
- **来源与时间**：arXiv · 2026-11-18T04:52:50+00:00
- **作者/分类**：Jiakun Yan、Shiksha Patel、David Sidler、Gengyu Rao、Brandon Potter、Bradford Beckmann · cs.DC、cs.LG
- **来源摘要**：Modern GPU workloads increasingly rely on device-initiated communication to let GPU kernels communicate directly without synchronizing with the host at kernel boundaries. Existing libraries commonly favor placing transport control on GPU threads, an approach known as GPUDirect Async \(GDA\), and treat reverse offload to host proxy threads as a slower fallback. We argue that reverse offload can outperform GDA and should be a first-class communication path. We further propose exposing reverse offload as a programming interface that lets users define workload-specific communication operations. We present KIWI, a programmable reverse-offload framework that achieves sub-microsecond one-way device-to-host latency and hundreds of millions of operations per second. We address practical challenges of scaling reverse offload, including contention on shared queues, polling overhead, and load imbalance across proxies. KIWI-P2P, a SHMEM-like library built on KIWI, outperforms state-of-the-art GDA implementations in both latency and bandwidth. More importantly, KIWI-EP redesigns low-latency mixture-of-experts dispatch and combine kernels around workload-defined operations, lowering expert-parallel communication latency by up to 2.3x over the best existing backend on AMD GPUs and time per output token in vLLM by up to 29%. The source code of KIWI is available at https://github.com/AMDResearch/kiwi.
