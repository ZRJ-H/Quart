### [DreamX：视频扩散模型作为通用潜在动作学习器](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05377v1)

- **原标题**：DreamX: Video Diffusion Model is a Universal Latent Action Learner
- **来源与时间**：arXiv · 2026-11-03T18:59:56+00:00
- **作者/分类**：Jisoo Kim、Chaehyun Kim、Junsu Kim、Jiho Park、Seongchan Kim、Eunbeen Hong、Soowon Son、Fu-En Yang、Seungryong Kim · cs.CV、cs.AI、cs.RO
- **核心摘要**：DreamX 将预训练视频扩散模型改用作通用潜在动作学习器，从视频模型的时空特征中提取紧凑潜在动作，把每个潜在视频帧从 1,200 个 token 压缩到 4 个，压缩约 300 倍。借助视频模型的生成先验，这些潜在动作捕捉与动作相关的动态，并与视觉外观解耦，从而构成跨人类与机器人、跨环境、跨动作参数化的共享动作接口。在 LARY-Bench 上，DreamX 在潜在动作模型中取得最优表现；在一个设定中编码的动作在另一设定中仍然有效，支持任意到任意的视频迁移以及跨具身、跨环境的零样本动作迁移。该共享接口还支持在紧凑潜在动作空间中进行策略学习，降低基于视频扩散的策略的计算成本。
- **研究问题**：如何从视频中学习在不同动作执行间一致、又对具身与外观变化不变的通用动作表示。
- **方法**：复用预训练视频扩散模型，从其时空特征中提取紧凑潜在动作，每帧压缩至 4 个 token，并利用生成先验使潜在动作与视觉外观解耦。
- **实验结果**：在 LARY-Bench 上取得潜在动作模型中的最优表现；支持任意到任意视频迁移与跨具身、跨环境零样本动作迁移；在紧凑潜在动作空间中做策略学习可降低视频扩散策略的计算成本。
- **局限**：本次采集的摘要未给出量化指标、失败案例或计算开销的具体数字。
- **工程价值**：潜在动作空间可作为跨具身共享接口，并降低基于视频扩散的策略的计算成本。
- **价值点**：把视频扩散模型复用为动作表示学习器，为跨具身共享动作接口提供了一条压缩率高、可复用的路径。

### [From Autonomous Agents to Cognitive Symbionts: Toward AI-Augmented Human Intelligence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05376v1)

- **原标题**：From Autonomous Agents to Cognitive Symbionts: Toward AI-Augmented Human Intelligence
- **来源与时间**：arXiv · 2026-11-03T18:59:52+00:00
- **作者/分类**：Hao Fei · cs.CL、cs.AI
- **来源摘要**：For a decade, progress in artificial intelligence has been measured by how much a machine can do on its own. Large foundation models now write code and run multistep tasks, and personal agents act on a person's behalf. Yet people still forget what they once knew, miss what they are not looking at, fail to find the right words, and face hard choices without the benefit of their own history, because the machine that could help stands outside the situation. As AI moves into the physical world, attention has settled on one kind of intelligent individual, the embodied robot; we argue that the era of Physical AI holds a second kind of equal importance, the person augmented by AI. Alongside artificial general intelligence, which asks how intelligent a machine can be, stands AI-augmented human intelligence \(AHI\), which asks how much intelligence a machine can enable in a particular person. Realizing it calls for a new kind of system, a cognitive symbiont: a lifelong, embedded, real-time, private, low-friction, and proactive companion that senses what the person senses through external devices, keeps their context across years, models their situation and their mind, takes part in their thinking at the right moment, carries out everyday tasks with their context, and keeps learning the person as both change. We give the paradigm a conceptual core, the cognitive gap between what a person could bring to a situation and what they can hold in working memory, and organize its design space as a symbiosis loop of sensing, keeping, understanding, living alongside, acting for and thinking with the person, and growing, enclosed by a personal runtime and by trust. Because a symbiont changes what a person can do in front of others, it also raises risks of a new kind, from covert assistance in examinations to pressure on everyone to adopt it, and we treat their governance as part of the paradigm.

- **实验结果**：AlpacaEval 2.0 上以 GPT-4o 为评判，长度控制胜率 Llama 从 31.7% 升至 45.3%，Mistral 从 6.8% 升至 18.4%；调优 8B 模型在所有报告胜率上高于 Llama 3.1 70B Instruct；同族开放权重标注器保留 62% 与 82% 的增益；消融变体低 6 至 10 个长度控制点，样本数与间隔扫描影响分别至多 2.1 与 2.4 点。
