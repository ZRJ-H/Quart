### [Sensorimotor Alignment: A Platonic Proxy for Embodied Policy Success](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31670v1)

- **原标题**：Sensorimotor Alignment: A Platonic Proxy for Embodied Policy Success
- **来源与时间**：arXiv · 2026-10-22T17:59:51+00:00
- **作者/分类**：Yunfei Xie、Howard H. Qian、Shiyi Lan、Kaiyu Hang、Chen Wei · cs.CV、cs.LG、cs.RO
- **来源摘要**：Robotic foundation models are typically evaluated through costly closed-loop rollouts, which motivates cheap offline proxies for model capability. The Platonic Representation Hypothesis \(PRH\) suggests that as representations improve, different modalities such as vision and language converge toward a shared statistical model of reality. We ask whether motion trajectories form another such modality: as embodied models become more capable, should their sensory representations converge toward the structure of physical motion? To examine this, we propose Sensorimotor Alignment \($S^2$\), a training-free offline metric that measures how well a model's sensory representation aligns with the geometry of physical motion. We find that $S^2$ correlates positively with downstream robot task success across embodied model families, making it a low-cost offline proxy for prioritizing models before closed-loop evaluation. $S^2$ can also identify promising vision-language model \(VLM\) backbones even before costly fine-tuning into vision-language-action models \(VLAs\). Beyond this correlation, $S^2$ offers representation-level explanations for phenomena observed in VLAs and world action models \(WAMs\). With $S^2$, we extend PRH to embodied models and make sensorimotor alignment empirically measurable, offering insight into why an embodied model succeeds beyond its closed-loop success rate.

### [Direct Pass@$k$ Optimization for Thought Pruning: Balancing Quality and Diversity in Test-Time Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31667v1)

- **原标题**：Direct Pass@$k$ Optimization for Thought Pruning: Balancing Quality and Diversity in Test-Time Reasoning
- **来源与时间**：arXiv · 2026-10-22T17:59:48+00:00
- **作者/分类**：Mahdi Salmani、Adel Javanmard、Devansh Gupta、Meisam Razaviyayn · cs.AI、cs.CL
- **来源摘要**：Test-time scaling has emerged as a key inference strategy for reasoning models, where a model generates a large number of candidate solutions in the hope of finding at least one correct solution. Running all candidate solutions to completion can be computationally demanding, so recent work prunes the candidate pool after generating only a short prefix, committing further computation only to the surviving candidates. Existing pruning strategies divide broadly into two categories: Quality-based methods assign a score to each partial solution and discard those predicted to ultimately lead to an incorrect final solution, while diversity-based methods select partial solutions that follow different reasoning trajectories and are therefore likely to cover different final solutions. The tension between these two objectives is an instance of exploration versus exploitation, yet little to no prior work on pruning exposes this trade-off directly, let alone optimizes it. In this paper, we introduce a pruning procedure that addresses this trade-off directly by optimizing pass@$k$ over the selected solution pool. In particular, it chooses the pool to maximize the probability that at least one of its partial solutions, when continued to completion, yields a correct answer. Because this objective simultaneously rewards high-quality partial solutions and penalizes redundancy among them, quality and diversity are naturally balanced by the criterion itself rather than through separate ad-hoc design choices. On eight open-weight reasoning models, our method outperforms existing quality-based and diversity-based pruning strategies. The gains are particularly pronounced when the pruning procedure keeps a moderate number of partial solutions for continued generation, highlighting the value of directly balancing quality and diversity during pruning.

### [从随机特征到全可训练网络的相变](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31663v1)

- **原标题**：Phase Transition from Random Features to Fully Trainable Networks
- **来源与时间**：arXiv · 2026-10-22T17:59:29+00:00
- **作者/分类**：Xin He、Huan Lao、Xiliang Lu、Shijun Zhang · math.NA、cs.LG、math.ST、stat.ML
- **核心摘要**：论文研究部分可训练神经网络在近似能力上的相变模式，这类网络介于固定随机特征与完全自适应表征之间。作者在多项式谱衰减指数 α&gt;1 与 leverage 相容性假设下，为宽度 m、可训练比例 P 的两层网络建立了平方 L2 近似速率 O\(\(κ\_P log\(m/δ\)/m\)^α\)，其中 κ\_P=\(P/κ\_1^\*+\(1-P\)/κ\_0^\*\)^{-1}，该界在 RKHS 单位球上以至少 1-δ 的概率一致成立；常数 κ\_0^\* 与 κ\_1^\* 分别量化固定组与可训练组相对目标 RKHS 的采样失配。对维度 d 的球面 biased-ReLU 特征，作者在初始化尺度 τ≥1 下验证假设，得到 α=\(d+2\)/\(d-1\)、κ\_0^\*=4√2τ\(d+1\)/d，并在 ridge leverage 采样下取 κ\_1^\*=1。数值上，d=5、τ=10 时约 4.1% 的可训练比例即可获得从 P=0 到 P=1 近似界下降量的 90%。训练误差呈现类似相变：先快速平滑改善，随后收益递减，初始化失配越大转变越尖锐；最多 15 层的网络实验也表现出类似相变。
- **研究问题**：部分可训练神经网络的近似能力如何随可训练比例变化，以及固定随机特征与全可训练网络之间是否存在可量化的相变。
- **方法**：在多项式谱衰减与 leverage 相容性假设下推导两层网络平方 L2 近似速率界，并在球面 biased-ReLU 特征上验证假设，辅以多设定数值实验。
- **实验结果**：得到含可训练比例 P 的近似速率 O\(\(κ\_P log\(m/δ\)/m\)^α\)；d=5、τ=10 时约 4.1% 可训练比例获得 90% 的近似界下降；训练误差呈现先快速改善后收益递减的相变，最多 15 层网络也有类似现象。
- **局限**：理论界依赖多项式谱衰减与 leverage 相容性等假设，并在特定特征与采样设定下验证；训练误差相变来自数值观察。
- **工程价值**：提示在部分可训练网络中，少量可训练参数即可捕获大部分近似收益，可用于设计可训练比例。
- **价值点**：把部分可训练网络的近似能力量化为相变式曲线，为在固定特征与全训练之间分配可训练参数提供理论依据。

- **来源与时间**：arXiv · 2026-10-22T17:59:22+00:00
- **作者/分类**：Isaac Gibbs、Elise L. Han、Ryan J. Tibshirani、Tijana Zrnic · stat.ML、cs.LG、stat.ME
- **来源摘要**：We consider online monitoring of an AI system that produces a sequence of decisions. At each round, the system outputs a proposed decision together with a confidence score estimating the trustworthiness of the decision. The decision and confidence score need not be produced by the same model: for example, one agent may propose a decision, while a separate verifier agent evaluates it and outputs a correctness or safety score. Based on this information, we must decide whether to accept the AI decision automatically or consult an expert, such as a human auditor, who reviews the proposal and either accepts or corrects it. We formulate this task as an online constrained optimization problem in which the goal is to minimize the frequency of expert auditing while guaranteeing that the fraction of AI errors that go unaudited stays below a user-defined threshold. We derive a family of algorithms with provable error-control guarantees with no assumptions, including a method that is optimally efficient: it asymptotically attains the best achievable auditing rate for both adversarial and i.i.d. data streams. Experiments on screening harmful requests to large language models, qualitative text annotation, and data labeling show that our methods achieve valid error control in practice while substantially reducing the need for human auditing compared to baselines.
