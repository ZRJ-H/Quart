### [Omni-Woodpecker: Hallucination Correction via Evidence-Prior Rebalancing for Omni LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27103)

- **原标题**：Omni-Woodpecker: Hallucination Correction via Evidence-Prior Rebalancing for Omni LLMs
- **来源与时间**：Hugging Face Daily Papers / arXiv · 2026-10-26T00:00:00+00:00
- **作者/分类**：Zijian Liu、Chaoyou Fu、Yan Yang、Caifeng Shan、Ran He · 来源未列出
- **来源摘要**：Recent Omni LLMs \(OLLMs\) have demonstrated remarkable capabilities in joint audio-visual reasoning, yet cross-modal hallucinations remain a major challenge. Existing mitigation methods mainly operate at the output level, overlooking the entanglement of grounding evidence and learned priors within fused representations. To address this issue, we propose Omni-Woodpecker \(OWP\), a training-free framework that shifts hallucination mitigation from output-level manipulation to conflict-conditioned representation rebalancing. Specifically, OWP first identifies cross-modal conflicts from complementary modality-specific views. It then employs orthogonal decomposition to separate grounding evidence from competing priors within the hidden states. Finally, OWP adaptively rebalances fused representations by suppressing misleading priors and restoring ignored evidence before decoding. Extensive experiments demonstrate the effectiveness and strong cross-architecture generalization of OWP. Using Qwen2.5-Omni-7B as the backbone, OWP improves performance by 12.9 and 8.6 points on CMM and AVHBench, respectively, while consistently achieving gains of 0.67-2.25 points across four general OLLM reasoning benchmarks. Furthermore, OWP generalizes effectively to VideoLLaMA2-AV, improving performance by 4.6 and 3.9 points on the same benchmarks. Code is publicly available at https://github.com/ZiiJianLiu/Omni-Woodpecker

### [Generalized CycleGRPO for Fine-Grained Multimodal Understanding and Grounding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29552)

- **原标题**：Generalized CycleGRPO for Fine-Grained Multimodal Understanding and Grounding
- **来源与时间**：Hugging Face Daily Papers / arXiv · 2026-10-26T00:00:00+00:00
- **作者/分类**：Xin Zhang、Haochen Wang、Beibei Lin、Zhaoxiang Zhang、Robby T. Tan、Jason Li、Dacheng Tao · 来源未列出
- **来源摘要**：Fine-grained multimodal intelligence requires models to both understand a designated region and localize it from language. Existing methods learn these directions with separate objectives and task-specific annotations. We present Generalized CycleGRPO, the first unified reinforcement learning framework coupling region understanding and localization across spatial, temporal, and spatio-temporal domains. Given a region, a multimodal large language model generates candidate descriptions and then localizes each description to reconstruct the input region. Reconstruction overlap provides a verifiable reward for captioning, while individual localization rewards improve the inverse mapping. A common region-space formulation extends this cycle from image masks and boxes to video intervals and timestamped spatial evidence, without reference captions or an external language-model judge during reinforcement learning. In videos, the cycle encourages descriptions that distinguish a target event from similar recurrences. We introduce DOVE \(Distinctive Occurrence-level Video-caption Evaluation\), a human-verified contrastive benchmark for this temporal distinctiveness. CycleGRPO improves SAMTok's overall gIoU from 57.5% to 68.2% on GroundingSuite and average gIoU from 71.2% to 78.7% on GRES. For temporal tasks, it achieves 51.9% tIoU on Charades and 33.3% Temporal Evidence Coverage on DOVE, compared with 45.6% and 14.8% for the Qwen3-VL initialization. These results establish region-text cycle consistency as a general self-evaluation principle for multimodal understanding and localization. Models, code, and DOVE are available at https://devinxzhang.github.io/Generalized-CycleGRPO-Page/.
