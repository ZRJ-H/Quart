### [Pruning Autonomous Driving VLAs: What to Preserve for Reasoning and Action](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31240v1)

- **原标题**：Pruning Autonomous Driving VLAs: What to Preserve for Reasoning and Action
- **来源与时间**：arXiv · 2026-11-23T18:59:52+00:00
- **作者/分类**：Seongchan Kim、Soowon Son、Sangoh Kim、Hyunah Ko、Eunbeen Hong、Hyunkoo Lee、Sanghyun Lee、WonJun Moon、Kihong Kim、Dongyeon Son、Woongchan Nam、Seungryong Kim · cs.CV、cs.LG、cs.RO
- **来源摘要**：Reasoning vision-language-action \(VLA\) models for autonomous driving use chain-of-thought \(CoT\) reasoning to guide action generation, but incur substantial memory and computational costs. We study structured pruning for these models, focusing on preserving both scene understanding and trajectory prediction. Our analysis of Taylor importance in Alpamayo-1.5-10B shows that reasoning and trajectory objectives assign different importance to the same vision-language model \(VLM\) structures. In the action expert, MLP channels exhibit more stable importance rankings across denoising steps than query heads, and a small fraction of channels accounts for most of the measured importance. Based on these findings, we propose MoOP, which combines importance ranks from both objectives for VLM pruning and aggregates stepwise importance to select a fixed subset of expert MLP channels. At 24.0% total parameter reduction, VLM-only pruning with MoOP performs comparably to or slightly better than the strongest baselines in driving, while retaining 94.0% of the dense model's LingoQA score, far above all other methods. At a higher reduction of 30.0%, MoOP degrades much less than every other method, as it is the only one whose reasoning does not collapse. Removing 93.75% of expert MLP channels increases total parameter reduction to 39.4%, with nearly unchanged open-loop errors relative to VLM-only pruning and competitive closed-loop performance. The resulting model achieves a 1.37x end-to-end inference speedup and 36.6% lower peak memory than the dense model, without post-pruning fine-tuning.

### [Let Vision-Language Models Generate Images through Their Representations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31237v1)

- **原标题**：Let Vision-Language Models Generate Images through Their Representations
- **来源与时间**：arXiv · 2026-11-23T18:59:32+00:00
- **作者/分类**：JB Kim、Byungwoo Jeon、Kyungmin Lee、Hyeonmo Kang、Cheonbok Park、Shinyoung Joo、Jinwoo Shin、Jeonghoon Kim · cs.CV、cs.LG
- **来源摘要**：Recent unified multimodal models aim to combine visual understanding and generation within a single model, yet effectively integrating the two capabilities remains challenging. Leveraging pretrained vision-language models offers a way to build on already learned multimodal knowledge, but making this knowledge effectively accessible to visual generation remains nontrivial. We find that a central challenge lies in the heterogeneous visual representations used for understanding and generation. To address this challenge, we introduce Integral, which converts a pretrained vision-language model into a unified multimodal model by constructing a shared visual representation space across the two capabilities. Integral implements this design through two complementary components. Abstraction Fusion constructs shared visual tokens by aggregating hierarchical representations from the frozen vision encoder, while Block-wise MoT introduces a lightweight generative stream that interacts with the vision-language model only at block boundaries. Our approach effectively preserves the pretrained understanding capability while extending it to strong visual generation. Controlled comparisons further demonstrate that sharing visual representations between understanding and generation substantially improves generative learning compared with using a separate visual representation space, despite comparable reconstruction fidelity. These results suggest that representations learned by pretrained vision-language models already provide an effective foundation for visual generation.

### [TowerBridge：用权重空间编程弥合双编码器模态鸿沟](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31233v1)

- **原标题**：TowerBridge: Bridging the Dual-Encoder Modality Gap via Weight-Space Programming
- **来源与时间**：arXiv · 2026-11-23T18:59:13+00:00
- **作者/分类**：Hani Alomari、Md Atabuzzaman、Chris Thomas · cs.CV、cs.LG
- **核心摘要**：CLIP 式双编码器把图像和文本映射到共享嵌入空间，但两种模态占据由模态鸿沟分隔的不同区域；当错误匹配或难负例排在正确匹配之前时检索失败，鸿沟常被视为原因，然而事后消除鸿沟会在所测试的每个 CLIP 检查点上降低检索性能。论文提出一种配对统计量，识别两个塔在难负例上比匹配对上更一致的方向，轻微衰减这些方向可改善检索且大体保留鸿沟。该修正通过权重空间编程得到：由统计量引导，一个 LLM 代理检查两个塔并写出闭式权重编辑，无需反向传播。
- **研究问题**：CLIP 式双编码器存在模态鸿沟，错误匹配或难负例可能排在正确匹配之前，而事后消除鸿沟反而降低检索性能。
- **方法**：提出配对统计量识别两塔在难负例上更一致的方向并轻微衰减；通过权重空间编程让 LLM 代理检查两塔并写出闭式权重编辑，无需反向传播；再将反复修正蒸馏为基于难负例边际一阶分析的 TowerBridge 闭式配方。
- **实验结果**：代理在全部 13 个 CLIP 检查点上改善 COCO 测试检索，12 个检查点编辑输出投影；TowerBridge 在全部 21 个评估检查点（含 8 个代理未见过的）上平均提升 COCO 测试检索 16.8 RSUM、Flickr30k 测试检索 6.6，ImageNet 零样本准确率下降不超过 0.5 点；在全部 21 个检查点上降低 hubness，并在其他家族 5 个双编码器中的 4 个上改善检索。
- **局限**：ImageNet 零样本准确率仍允许最多 0.5 点下降，且在其他家族双编码器中有一个未获改善。
- **工程价值**：配方由 25,000 个带标签对在数秒内算出，无需模型特定调参，不改变架构与推理成本，便于直接用于已有 CLIP 检查点。
- **价值点**：论文区分了模态鸿沟与难负例排序问题，并给出可复用的闭式修正，对双编码器检索改进有直接参考价值。

### [How Far Can Linear Go? BiPert, a Unified Framework for Single-Cell Perturbation Modelling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31227v1)

- **原标题**：How Far Can Linear Go? BiPert, a Unified Framework for Single-Cell Perturbation Modelling
- **来源与时间**：arXiv · 2026-11-23T18:58:32+00:00
- **作者/分类**：Abhishek Sharma、Aleksej Zelezniak · cs.LG、q-bio.CB、q-bio.GN、q-bio.QM
- **来源摘要**：Understanding how genetic perturbations affect cellular behaviour is essential for studying gene function and regulatory mechanisms. Large-scale CRISPR screens measure these effects across thousands of genes, but cover relatively few cellular contexts. Predicting perturbation responses in unseen cell types and identifying perturbed genes from their transcriptional effects therefore remain challenging. Here, we show that both tasks can be addressed through a common linear decomposition of transcriptional responses into shared and perturbation-specific components. Building on this principle, we introduce BiPert, a simple linear framework for both forward perturbation prediction and inverse identification. For forward prediction, BiPert outperforms existing methods that use no external biological knowledge when predicting gene expression changes in unseen cell lines. Its few-shot extension, BiPert-FS, adapts to new cell lines using as few as ten measured perturbations. For inverse identification, BiPert-ID identifies perturbed genes from single-cell expression profiles, outperforming deep-learning methods in unseen cell lines and donors. We further show that perturbations whose effects transfer more consistently across cellular contexts are also easier to identify, even after controlling for perturbation effect size. Together, these results establish a connection between perturbation transfer and identification, and demonstrate that simple linear models provide strong, interpretable baselines for both tasks.
