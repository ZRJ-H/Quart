### [OmniAct: One Policy, One Million Embodiments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33158v1)

- **原标题**：OmniAct: One Policy, One Million Embodiments
- **来源与时间**：arXiv · 2026-11-24T18:59:58+00:00
- **作者/分类**：Xiang Fan、Yuheng Wang、Jiayuan Yan、Bohan Fang、Wisdom Oluchi Ikezogwo、Ainaz Eftekhar、Max Argus、Jason Ren、Jiafei Duan、Ranjay Krishna · cs.RO、cs.AI、cs.CV、cs.LG
- **来源摘要**：A single policy that controls diverse robots must not only transfer task knowledge across embodiments, but also adapt its behavior to each robot's capabilities. Existing approaches facilitate transfer through shared action representations, but these representations do not necessarily capture how differences in robot morphology should shape manipulation strategies. We introduce OmniAct, a cross-embodiment manipulation policy that conditions directly on each robot's kinematics in a specific scene to produce embodiment-specific behavior. This kinematic graph represents the robot's joint positions, motion axes, and connectivity to its visual observation, allowing the policy to account for how its embodiment related to objects and surfaces in the scene. A flow-matching policy predicts relative joint motions for each node, enabling control across robots with different degrees of freedom and kinematic configurations. To learn how physical structure shapes behavior at scale, we procedurally design and filter one million physically feasible robot embodiments; next, we develop algorithms to generate robot trajectories in simulation for all embodiments; finally, we train across diverse simulated manipulation tasks. On 2,000 embodiment-scene-held-out evaluation episodes, OmniAct achieves 63.0% success, compared to 17.8% for pi 0.5 and 57.9% for the strongest baseline; on the task families GPT-6 Astra was evaluated on, it achieves 64.7% against GPT-6 Astra's 41.1%. On a previously unseen real-world robot, it achieves nearly twice the success rate of the next best method, MolmoAct2. Beyond task success, we observe that OmniAct learns distinct manipulation strategies that exploit the capabilities and accommodate the limitations of different morphologies. Project: https://omniact.github.io/

- **来源摘要**：We present DiaCap, an audiovisual LLM for comprehensive dialogue captioning, which produces the transcript, timestamps, and a visual description of the speaker for every utterance in a video. Considering that utterance timing and audiovisual diarization are tightly coupled, DiaCap unifies the tasks in a single model, trained in two complementary stages. First, we train DiaCap on 225K curated clips, using synthetic captions that densely label every utterance in detail. ### [BioLoom：利用科学文章结构扩展生物医学视觉语言模型](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33151v1)

- **原标题**：BioLoom: Scaling Biomedical Vision-Language Models by Learning from the Structure of Scientific Articles
- **来源与时间**：arXiv · 2026-11-24T18:59:04+00:00
- **作者/分类**：Yuwei Yang、Xiaoxiao Sun、Alejandro Lozano、Yuan-Sen Ting、Yuhui Yuan、Liang Zheng、Serena Yeung-Levy · cs.CV、cs.LG
- **核心摘要**：BioLoom 关注生物医学视觉语言模型的对比式图文预训练，作者提出除扩大数据规模外，还可从现有数据中学习更丰富的表示。其假设是科学文献本身的结构提供额外监督信号：图、子图、图注和正文段落编码了跨模态的丰富关系，而把它们当作独立图文对会忽略这些关系。为此，作者在对比图文监督中保留精确图文匹配，同时为相关对引入较弱的正监督，例如子图与同级子图的图注；对同内容类型的跨文章负样本加权，以鼓励更细的区分；并用视觉内容、原始图注和提及段落丰富子图描述。将子图与这些描述配对、整图与原始图注配对，得到 5,240 万图文对的 Loom52M 数据集，覆盖科学图表和生物医学图像。使用该数据集，BioLoom（DFN-2B）在 10 个领域 43 个数据集的 67 个零样本分类任务上取得 56.82% 的领域均衡平均准确率，MedPMC-CLIP 为 48.77%；在 BioLoomBench 和 BioMedFlickr 上双向平均 Recall@1 分别为 63.20% 和 10.59%，对应最强基线 BiomedCLIP 和 MedPMC-CLIP 为 46.37% 和 7.55%。
- **研究问题**：对比式图文预训练主要依赖扩大数据规模，而把文献中的图、子图、图注和正文当作独立图文对，会忽略它们之间的结构关系。
- **方法**：在对比监督中保留精确图文匹配，为相关对引入较弱正监督，对同内容类型的跨文章负样本加权，并用视觉内容、原始图注和提及段落丰富子图描述；构建 5,240 万图文对的 Loom52M 数据集训练 BioLoom（DFN-2B）。
- **实验结果**：在 10 个领域 43 个数据集的 67 个零样本分类任务上领域均衡平均准确率 56.82%，MedPMC-CLIP 为 48.77%；BioLoomBench 和 BioMedFlickr 上双向平均 Recall@1 为 63.20% 和 10.59%，最强基线分别为 46.37% 和 7.55%。

- **工程价值**：代码、数据集、基准和模型均已公开，Loom52M 与结构感知的监督设计可直接用于生物医学图文预训练。

### [CoT-Proxy: Cross-Format Transfer for Distilling Long-Horizon Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33148v1)

- **原标题**：CoT-Proxy: Cross-Format Transfer for Distilling Long-Horizon Policies
- **来源与时间**：arXiv · 2026-11-24T18:58:53+00:00
- **作者/分类**：Dawid J. Kopiczko、Tijmen Blankevoort、Yuki M. Asano · cs.CL
- **来源摘要**：Reasoning models become long-horizon agents through reinforcement learning or, more cheaply, through fine-tuning on a stronger teacher's trajectories, which contain the teacher's chain-of-thought as well as its actions. The standard fine-tuning approach imitates both, yet the strongest teachers are closed models whose chain-of-thought is hidden behind an API. We show that the chain-of-thought can be left out altogether and that the long-horizon policy can be distilled from the teacher's actions alone. The student is trained on these trajectories with its reasoning block left empty, while a KL loss on a replay buffer of its own generations limits drift from its initial model. Surprisingly, the policy learned from these trajectories with an empty reasoning block carries over to inference with thinking enabled, where the student reasons in its own style while acting like the teacher, an effect we call cross-format transfer. Distilling the actions of Qwen3.6-27B raises the task success of Qwen3-4B on AppWorld from 7% to 18% and that of Qwen3-32B from 12% to 25%. We further introduce CoT-proxies, short summaries of the teacher's reasoning placed after the empty reasoning block, which raise Qwen3-4B further, to 35%. Since neither actions nor CoT-proxies need the hidden chain-of-thought, the recipe applies unchanged to closed teachers. Distilling GPT-5.6 Luna from its visible outputs and the reasoning summaries its API returns raises Qwen3-4B from 7% to 48% on AppWorld and from 3% to 28% on SWE-bench Verified.

### [iWAM: Efficient Action Prediction from World Models without Explicit Future Prediction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33141v1)

- **原标题**：iWAM: Efficient Action Prediction from World Models without Explicit Future Prediction
- **来源与时间**：arXiv · 2026-11-24T18:56:04+00:00
- **作者/分类**：Vansh Kapoor、Max Sobol Mark、Sreyas Venkataraman、Max Simchowitz、Aviral Kumar · cs.RO、cs.CV、cs.LG
- **来源摘要**：World-action models \(WAMs\) build policies on pretrained video-generation models, on the premise that predicting how a scene evolves helps determine how to act. However, generating future scenes is expensive for real-time control. This motivates efficient proxies for future imagination, but how to train such proxies without sacrificing action-prediction quality remains unclear. An effective proxy need not generate a high-resolution frame; it only needs to provide enough information to distinguish between possible futures. This leads to the design principle behind iWAM: rather than denoising an entire future frame, can we condition the action policy on intermediate features from a single video-model evaluation? We show that the answer is yes. Exploiting the reversible dynamics of flow-matching models, we invert each training future into the corresponding latent noise vector. However, a single velocity evaluation at this latent does not necessarily yield informative features for action prediction. We therefore learn a bidirectional flow map that amortizes future-generation computation, enabling a single model evaluation to provide effective conditioning features. At inference, we sample a latent noise vector from the prior and condition the policy using just one video-backbone forward pass. Across simulated benchmarks and real robots, iWAM recovers the generalization benefits of WAMs while substantially reducing its inference cost.
