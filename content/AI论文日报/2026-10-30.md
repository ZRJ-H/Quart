### [厘清语言模型数据归因的设计空间](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40655v1)

- **原标题**：Elucidating the Design Space of LM Data Attribution
- **来源与时间**：arXiv · 2026-10-29T17:59:53+00:00
- **作者/分类**：Steven Yu、Sheng-Yu Wang、Kimia Hamidieh、Antonio Torralba、David Bau · cs.CL
- **核心摘要**：论文聚焦语言模型数据归因方法的评测问题：现有评测同时改变多项数据集属性，难以判断归因算法失败的具体原因。作者构建受控归因基准 CATT，用完全虚构的知识微调语言模型，从而获得无歧义的事实溯源真值，并可逐项调整文档冗余、词汇干扰项等配置。实验显示冗余和干扰项会持续降低归因表现。作者没有提出新的估计器，而是考察梯度所用损失函数与逐 token 分数聚合方式两个设计选择，发现训练文档中真正的因果证据高度局部化，而标准方法对整篇文档取平均会把证据稀释为噪声；改用负对数几率梯度替代标准交叉熵梯度后，关键 token 得以凸显，只聚合最高 token 分数可在多个基线上将真值召回率提升 35 至 72 个百分点。该负对数几率修正还可迁移到 FTRACE-TREX，使所测每种方法的反事实重训练表现最多提升 4.9 倍。

- **方法**：构建 CATT 受控归因基准，用虚构知识微调模型以获得明确真值，并逐项改变文档冗余、词汇干扰项等配置；考察计算梯度所用损失与逐 token 分数聚合两个设计选择，用负对数几率梯度替代交叉熵梯度并仅聚合最高 token 分数。
- **实验结果**：冗余和干扰项持续降低归因表现；负对数几率梯度能凸显关键 token，仅聚合最高 token 分数使多个基线的真值召回率提升 35 至 72 个百分点；该修正迁移到 FTRACE-TREX 后使所测每种方法的反事实重训练表现最多提升 4.9 倍。

### [Propose-Filter-Release: Scaling Noise to Typical Sensitivity via Private Filtering](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40654v1)

- **原标题**：Propose-Filter-Release: Scaling Noise to Typical Sensitivity via Private Filtering
- **来源与时间**：arXiv · 2026-10-29T17:59:49+00:00
- **作者/分类**：Moshe Shenfeld、Eliad Tsfadia、Katrina Ligett · cs.LG
- **来源摘要**：An estimator can be accurate and stable on typical data, yet sensitive to a few possible records. Differentially private mechanisms commonly scale their noise to the worst-case influence of a single record, which lets these records dictate the noise even when the remaining data would allow far less. Removing them first would restore the smaller scale, but the removal must itself be private. We introduce \\emph{propose-filter-release} \(PFR\), which, by privately filtering its input, turns a mechanism that protects the addition or removal of a typical record, one that fits the remaining data, into one that is differentially private in the standard sense, on every input. Using our novel filtering step, PFR requires only a concave friendliness score, which measures how well a record fits a dataset, and a release mechanism that is friendly-DP, that is, private when the changed record fits the remaining data. For every such score and release mechanism, it guarantees differential privacy under public conditions on the number of records, so it is a general privacy primitive, comparable to propose-test-release, and FriendlyCore can be viewed as one of its implementations. We illustrate the generality of PFR by instantiating it for scalar statistics, principal subspace estimation, the KL divergence of user histograms, the effective resistances of a graph, and treatment-rule selection.

### [Rethinking Neural Thickets Without Weight Perturbations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40649v1)

- **原标题**：Rethinking Neural Thickets Without Weight Perturbations
- **来源与时间**：arXiv · 2026-10-29T17:59:29+00:00
- **作者/分类**：Nicolas Dufour、Elouan Gardès、Amil Dravid、Patrick Pérez、Loic Landrieu · cs.LG、cs.AI、cs.CV
- **来源摘要**：The Neural Thickets hypothesis posits that pretrained language models lie in dense neighborhoods of task-specific experts in weight space, which random Gaussian perturbations can uncover. We reproduce the reported gains, but show that they do not require this geometric explanation. Perturbing a single decoder block already recovers much of the improvement, while keeping the model entirely frozen and adding a random bias to its output logits matches or exceeds full-weight perturbations on most reasoning tasks. This suggests an alternative explanation based on output diversity, validation-based selection, and voting. We formalize this view as Perturb-Select-Vote \(PSV\), a general procedure that generates perturbed predictors, selects high-performing members on a small validation set, and aggregates their predictions. This interpretation connects Neural Thickets to established inference-time methods, including sampling, self-consistency, and verifier-guided selection. A first-order analysis further shows that small weight perturbations act primarily through their induced logit shifts. We extend this analysis to vision, where logit PSV substantially improves performance under domain shift, including on Earth observation, while full-weight perturbations provide little additional benefit. Together, these results suggest that the gains attributed to nearby weight-space experts can instead be obtained through output diversity, selection, and voting. Logit PSV realizes these benefits with a fully frozen model, providing a simple, training-free alternative to full-weight perturbation ensembles across language and vision.

- **作者/分类**：Jiongfan Yang、Weichen Fan、Haiwen Diao、Zhongang Cai、Ziwei Liu · cs.CV、cs.AI、cs.LG、cs.NE
- **核心摘要**：论文针对像素空间生成 Transformer 通常每个图像块使用一个 token、细节越细 token 越多且全局注意力开销越大的问题，提出 SpecSlot。其观察是更细的细节不一定需要更多全局通信：低频结构适合全局交换，高频细节可在局部建模。SpecSlot 是像素空间 rectified-flow Transformer，将每个空间锚点扩展为多个槽位，每个槽位分配一组不相交的 DCT 系数频带，从而把锚点密度与频谱细分解耦；只有每个锚点的最低频槽位参与全局注意力，所有槽位在局部交互。空间头进一步为频谱预测加入局部修正，生成状态、目标和损失仍保持在像素空间。在 ImageNet-256 上，SpecSlot 取得与 FLOPs 匹配的 JiT 对照相当的最终 FID，同时收敛更快；在 ImageNet-512 上，其 FID 比对照改善 2.84，FLOPs 少 3.9%，并以低 38.2% 的成本接近 JiT-B/16 的 FID，差距仅 0.69。在文本到图像生成中，SpecSlot 在 DPG-Bench 上超过 JiT-B/8 达 1.06，前向成本低 40.6%。
- **价值点**：提出把全局通信限制在低频槽位、高频细节局部建模的像素空间生成架构，为在不扩大全局序列的前提下提升频谱细节提供了具体方案。

- **核心摘要**：论文讨论分布鲁棒优化（DRO）如何为数据驱动两阶段随机规划对冲有限记录带来的分布误差，并指出标准模糊集对记录中未出现实现的处理不同：围绕经验分布的散度球只包含记录的重加权，而传输球还包含记录之外支撑的分布，甚至包括数据生成过程无法产生的实现。作者改为以拟合记录的密度模型为中心构建 KL 球，即学习到的名义分布，使对手可按模型赋予的概率上调代价高昂的未观测实现。求解该问题存在第二重困难：问题只能容纳少量场景，而同等规模的独立样本很可能遗漏最坏情况集中的稀有实现。因此作者自适应构建场景集，在求解有限问题与从学习名义分布抽取新实现之间交替，抽取朝当前决策下的最坏情况引导，并用重要性权重把抽样校正回模型。在多物品报童问题和基于真实电网数据的容量扩展问题上，当记录稀缺且模型类能捕捉可外推的结构时，以学习名义分布而非记录为中心可降低分布偏移下的成本，且分布内成本相当；随着记录增多或偏移偏离模型学到的结构，这一优势减弱。
