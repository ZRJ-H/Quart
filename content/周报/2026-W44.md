  - [UserArena：面向长程购物智能体的交互式用户模拟基准](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30932)
    - 摘要：论文提出 UserArena，一个真实电商交互式基准，包含来自 11,010 名用户的 64,729 条会话轨迹，覆盖 566,102 个商品和 13 种动作类型。该基准将轨迹与交互环境配对，并支持对照一次受控生产 A/B 测试的结果做离线验证，评估分为输出有效性、局部决策、序列行为、个性化与最终结果五个层级。为提升长程交互中的模拟器保真度，作者提出 On-Policy Trajectory Distillation（OPTD），利用轨迹级意图在学生模型生成的 rollout 上提供稠密教师目标。实验显示，在 UserArena 评估协议下，OPTD 在所评估的模拟器中取得最强整体保真度，并在离线模拟中最接近复现生产策略效果。 结果：在 UserArena 评估协议下，OPTD 在所评估的模拟器中整体保真度最强，并在离线模拟中最接近复现生产策略效果。
  - [Omni-Woodpecker: Hallucination Correction via Evidence-Prior Rebalancing for Omni LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27103)
    - 摘要：Recent Omni LLMs \(OLLMs\) have demonstrated remarkable capabilities in joint audio-visual reasoning, yet cross-modal hallucinations remain a major challenge. Existing mitigation methods mainly operate at the output level, overlooking the entanglement of grounding evidence and learned priors within fused representations. To address this issue, we propose Omni-Woodpecker \(OWP\), a training-free framework that shifts hallucination mitigation from output-level manipulation to conflict-conditioned representation rebalancing. Specifically, OWP first identifies cross-modal conflicts from complementary modality-specific views. It then employs orthogonal decomposition to separate grounding evidence from competing priors within the hidden states. Finally, OWP adaptively rebalances fused representations by suppressing misleading priors and restoring ignored evidence before decoding. Extensive experiments demonstrate the effectiveness and strong cross-architecture generalization of OWP. Using Qwen2.5-Omni-7B as the backbone, OWP improves performance by 12.9 and 8.6 points on CMM and AVHBench, respectively, while consistently achieving gains of 0.67-2.25 points across four general OLLM reasoning benchmarks. Furthermore, OWP generalizes effectively to VideoLLaMA2-AV, improving performance by 4.6 and 3.9 points on the same benchmarks. Code is publicly available at https://github.com/ZiiJianLiu/Omni-Woodpecker
  - [Echo：面向视觉语言动作模型的耦合动作与任务状态信号去噪](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31569)
    - 摘要：论文关注机器人识别任务完成与失败的能力，使机器人在成功后停止、失败后恢复或重新规划。现有方法为视觉语言动作（VLA）策略增加任务状态信号预测，但往往需要细粒度监督、外部监控模型或复杂架构，且任务状态信号直接引导动作生成的潜力尚未充分探索。Echo 将停止与失败两种信号作为每个动作块的两个维度，与机器人动作一起去噪，其组合可区分执行中、成功完成和失败三种状态。为免去帧级进度标注，Echo 构建成功、失败和恢复轨迹，利用其互补的监督性质学习信号；同时控制信号与动作的交互，并让信号比动作更快去噪，使后续动作去噪步骤能基于更干净的状态估计，同时保持信号预测准确。真实机器人操作实验显示，Echo 能准确预测任务状态并持续提升策略性能。 结果：真实机器人操作实验显示，Echo 能准确预测任务状态，并持续提升策略性能。
