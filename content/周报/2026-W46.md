  - [论 ReLU 网络集合的非闭性](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09887v1)
    - 摘要：论文证明：对有界连通开集 Ω⊂R^d，至多 N 个神经元的单隐藏层 ReLU 网络集合在 W^{1,p}\(Ω\) 中是弱闭的。由此，该空间上每个强制性、弱下半连续的泛函都能在集合上取到最小值，覆盖凸被积函数的 Deep Ritz 方法、Sobolev 训练和 Lipschitz 学习。论文还证明，当权重衰减参数趋于零时，正则化问题的极小点（沿子列）收敛到参数范数最小的极小点。另一方面，作者证明两个隐藏层、宽度有界的 ReLU 网络集合在 W^{1,p}\(Ω\) 和 C\(Ω 闭包\) 中都不闭，这否定了 Petersen、Raslan 和 Voigtlaender 的一个猜想。 结果：单隐藏层 ReLU 网络集合弱闭，使强制性、弱下半连续能量可取到最小值，覆盖凸被积函数的 Deep Ritz 方法、Sobolev 训练和 Lipschitz 学习；权重衰减趋于零时极小点沿子列收敛到参数范数最小的极小点；双隐藏层有界宽度网络集合在 W^{1,p}\(Ω\) 与 C\(Ω 闭包\) 中不闭，否定 Petersen、Raslan 和 Voigtlaender 的猜想。

  - [Guiding Data Allocation for Robust Subpopulation Generalization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12884v1)
    - 摘要：Machine learning models often achieve high average accuracy in the training data while performing poorly on test data due to subpopulation shift. A common data-centric solution is to enforce balanced subgroup proportions, but full balance can be costly, infeasible, and not always necessary. In this work, we study whether balanced training data is the unique optimal configuration for robust subpopulation generalization. We extend a Gaussian feature-model framework to theoretically characterize the performance landscape across general training subgroup distributions. We show that multiple configurations, including substantially imbalanced ones, can perform comparably to the balanced one.   - [Kolmogorov-Chapman Maps: Trajectory-Free Learning for Simulating Molecular Dynamics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12880v1)
    - 摘要：Stochastic differential equations \(SDEs\) describe stochastic processes across a wide range of scientific domains, including physics, biology, and finance. Although individual numerical integration steps are inexpensive, observing meaningful events or transitions can require millions of sequential steps, resulting in substantial wall-clock simulation time and poor hardware utilization. Deep learning offers an alternative by amortizing many integration steps into a single neural network forward pass, potentially enabling much faster simulation. However, existing supervised approaches typically require long simulated trajectory data, which restricts their applicability. In this work, we introduce Kolmogorov-Chapman Maps \(KC-Maps\), a method for learning the transition kernel of an SDE that relies only on the trajectory-free marginal samples together with the known drift and diffusion coefficients. Given an initial time and state of a process, KC-Maps models its distribution integrated to any given time, corresponding to the weak solution of the SDE. To recover the correct dynamics, KC-Maps satisfies two key properties: \(i\) it satisfies the boundary conditions, which we incorporate directly into the neural network architecture, and \(ii\) it respects the Kolmogorov--Chapman equation, which provides an efficient training objective. We empirically demonstrate that KC-Maps learns to integrate the dynamics of peptide molecular systems and outperforms classical molecular dynamics in terms of GPU-hours.
  - [Adaptive Quantization Preserves Fast Rates for TD Learning over Rate-Limited Channels](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12878v1)
    - 摘要：Motivated by reinforcement learning subject to communication constraints, we study a policy evaluation problem with linear function approximation over a rate-limited channel. In our setup, an agent interacting with an unknown environment \(modeled as a Markov Decision Process\) transmits quantized estimates of a temporal-difference \(TD\) learning update direction to a server over a finite-capacity channel. Our goal is to investigate to what extent one can preserve the statistical convergence rates achievable when the channel has infinite capacity. To that end, we propose a new algorithm called Batched Adaptively Quantized TD \(BAQ-TD\), which uses batching to reduce the variance of the transmitted information, and adaptive quantization to encode innovations. We prove a high-probability finite-time error bound for BAQ-TD, and show that, above a certain finite channel capacity, BAQ-TD preserves the convergence rate of unquantized TD up to logarithmic factors. Specifically, for a $d$-dimensional value function parameter, we establish that transmitting a total of $\\mathcal{O}\(d \\log\(T\)\)$ bits over a horizon of length $T$ suffices to achieve the order-optimal rate of $\\tilde{\\mathcal{O}}\(1/T\)$, marking an exponential improvement in communication-complexity over prior work.
