  - [Energy-Based Reasoners: Your Recurrent Reasoner Should Be an Energy-Based Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08977v1)
    - 摘要：Looping is a powerful inductive bias for reasoning. By repeatedly iterating a recurrent block, looped models can scale their effective depth and enable multi-step reasoning in latent space. However, existing looped models are not inherently guided by an explicit measure of progress, making it difficult to determine when to stop looping and how to improve upon the current state. To enable this, we introduce Energy-Based Reasoners \(EBRs\), which learn an energy function over latent reasoning states and formulate looped updates as gradient descent on this energy. The energy scores latent states, while its gradient guides refinement and provides a signal for stopping. To train EBRs effectively, we further introduce exploration-based training to discover additional modes and to better shape the energy landscape over a broader set of states. Across four algorithmic reasoning benchmarks, EBRs match or outperform prior recurrent reasoning models, achieving 99.9% accuracy on Sudoku-Extreme while using 345$\\times$ less inference compute than the next-best model and maintaining comparable training compute. On all multi-solution benchmarks, EBRs achieve state-of-the-art coverage of distinct valid solutions while maintaining near-100% single-sample accuracy. These results suggest that energy-based models are a promising approach for strong reasoning performance.
  - [超越截断？蕴含许可的删减与 LLM 解码中语义保持的限度](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08975v1)
    - 摘要：论文研究能否在解码循环内用免训练控制器删除指令微调 LLM 超出请求所需的多余内容，并以把模型自身答案截断到相同长度作为最简单基线。一个熵门控的 logits 处理器在 MT-Bench 开发问题上删除 25.5%–34.9% 的 token，在封存测试集上删除 19.9%–34.2%，处于预先注册的 BERTScore 非劣效边界内；但作者证明并经审计确认，其输出始终是未修改答案的前缀，节省实际来自截断。对 23 类免训练机制的调查发现，在没有 oracle 标签的情况下，没有一类能比截断更好地选择内容。作者因此提出 ELIDE：在每个句子边界，仅当自然语言推理模型判定某段文本已被此前展示的文本蕴含时，才不向读者展示该段，同时不改变模型的生成。ELIDE 在三个模型家族上优于等长截断，但阅读 670 条公开提示上的 936 次删减后发现，59% 的删减损害答案，且没有自动指标检测到这一失败。把许可范围限定到话语单元后，在两个新测试上，有害删减比例降至约十分之一。 结果：熵门控方法删除 25.5%–34.9%（MT-Bench 开发集）和 19.9%–34.2%（封存测试集）的 token，但输出恒为原答案前缀；23 类机制中无一在无 oracle 标签时优于截断；ELIDE 在三个模型家族上优于等长截断，但 936 次删减中 59% 损害答案且无自动指标检出；限定话语单元后有害比例降至约十分之一。
  - [Phantom Consensus: Measuring Social Reasoning in Multi-Agent LLM Decision-Making](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08971v1)
    - 摘要：Multi-agent LLM systems increasingly aggregate judgments across model instances, but reasoning about other agents can itself introduce failure. We study a setting in which LLMs shift their public decisions after observing ambiguous social signals while reporting inaccurate estimates of what other agents privately judged, even when the group's recorded private judgments do not support the apparent consensus. We call this failure phantom consensus and study it as a pluralistic-ignorance-style pattern in controlled LLM collectives. To measure this phenomenon, we introduce PHANTOM-Bench, a benchmark that separates isolated private judgments, public decisions after ambiguous social signals, elicited estimates of the group's private judgment, transparency-based recovery, and controls for neutral public text and explicit vote-following. Across nine closed- and open-weight models, ambiguous public signals consistently degrade decision quality and can turn correct private group judgments into incorrect public collective outcomes. Public accuracy drops by as much as 47.8 percentage points, and collective rationality failures reach 47.3% in the most susceptible setting. We observe substantial errors in reported group-private beliefs alongside public-stage failures, while control conditions reveal model-dependent differences in susceptibility to ambiguous and explicit peer signals. Revealing the agents' recorded private decisions repairs many failures without revealing the gold label, although recovery varies across models. Our results show that evaluating multi-agent LLM systems requires measuring not only final agreement, but whether that agreement reflects independently measured private judgments or an apparent consensus.
